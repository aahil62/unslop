# Client Intake Questionnaire

The questions to answer **before any design or code** — whether you're the agent
interviewing the owner, or a freelancer interviewing a paying client. Skip
nothing high-impact; don't ask what's already answered. Each section ends with
what it locks.

> Tip for freelancers: run this on a 30-minute call, fill it in live, and send it
> back as a one-page "project brief." That document alone makes you look senior.

---

## 1. Project & goals

1. What is this site for? (sell a product · book appointments · generate leads ·
   showcase work · inform)
2. What is the **one action** a visitor should take? (buy · book · call · sign up)
3. Who is the visitor? Describe one real person.
4. What does success look like in 30 days? (X bookings, Y signups…)
5. Hard constraints — deadline, budget, existing brand, must-have integrations?

**Locks:** purpose · primary call-to-action · audience · success metric · constraints.

---

## 2. Look & feel

Pick a starting aesthetic — these map to the template's theme presets:

| Preset | Feel | Good for |
|--------|------|----------|
| **Organic Tech** | Calm, clinical, premium (moss/clay/cream) | wellness, boutique services, health |
| **Midnight Luxe** | Dark, editorial, expensive (obsidian/champagne) | agencies, luxury, high-ticket |
| **Brutalist Signal** | Bold, high-contrast, confident (paper/signal-red) | creators, events, statements |
| **Vapor Clinic** | Neon, futuristic, techy (deep void/plasma) | AI/SaaS, dev tools, crypto |

6. Which preset is closest? (we tune from there — it's not locked forever)
7. Any 2–3 reference sites you love? What specifically about each?
8. Light, dark, or both?
9. Existing logo / colors / fonts, or starting fresh?

**Locks:** theme preset · references · light/dark · brand assets.

---

## 3. Pages & content

10. One page or several? If several, list them (Home, Services, About, Contact…).
11. For the landing, which sections? (hero · features · social proof · pricing ·
    FAQ · CTA · footer)
12. Who writes the words — them or you? Is copy ready, or do we draft it?
13. Real images/logos available, or do we need stock/generated assets?
14. Social proof on hand? (testimonials, logos, numbers, reviews)

**Locks:** sitemap · section list per page · copy owner · asset plan · proof.

---

## 4. Tech & integrations

15. Does it need a **contact or lead form**? Where should submissions go?
    (email · spreadsheet · CRM)
16. **Booking/scheduling**? (Calendly embed · custom)
17. **Payments**? (Stripe checkout · just link out · none for v1)
18. **Content the owner edits later**? (a CMS) or is it fine for you to update?
19. Custom **domain**? Do they own one, or need to buy?
20. Analytics — do they want to see visitor numbers?

**Locks:** form destination · booking · payments · CMS vs hardcoded · domain · analytics.

---

## 5. Launch & handoff

21. Verification: it must build clean, look right at 375 / 768 / 1024 / 1440px,
    and the primary action must actually work end-to-end.
22. Where does it live? (Vercel — free tier is fine for most)
23. After launch, who maintains it? Do they want a walkthrough recording?

**Locks:** acceptance checks · hosting · maintenance + handoff plan.

---

## Scope → tier (for freelancers)

Use the answers to drop the job into a tier instead of guessing a price:

| Tier | Looks like | Signals from above |
|------|-----------|--------------------|
| **Starter** | One-page landing, 1 form, their copy | §3 = single page, §4 mostly "none" |
| **Standard** | Multi-page, forms + booking, light copy help | several pages, 1–2 integrations |
| **Premium** | Multi-page, payments/CMS, copy + assets done for them | payments or CMS, you own copy + assets |

> Set your own numbers per tier — but always quote the **tier**, never hourly.
> Clients buy outcomes, not hours.
